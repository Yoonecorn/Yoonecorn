### A little help from AI

I use AI coding agents as part of my development workflow, experimenting with different models, independent cross-model reviews, and iterative testing.

Different models have different strengths and blind spots. I find that having them challenge one another can be useful, but the final judge is always the code, the benchmarks, and the underlying biology.

### Operating principles

- **Respect the assay:** High-depth sequencing won't resurrect poorly lysed cells.
- **Deterministic always:** Same input, different output? Straight to jail.
- **Trust, but verify:** AI can write the code, but it doesn't get to grade its own homework.
- **Ground truth:** P-values can be flattered; raw reads and cash balances don't lie.
- **Respect the tail risk:** Don't bet the farm on unhedged spreads or unvalidated sgRNAs.
